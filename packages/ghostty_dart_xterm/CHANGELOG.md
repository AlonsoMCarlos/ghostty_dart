## 0.0.1-dev

- Unreleased. Package skeleton of the `package:xterm` 4.0.0 compatible
  adapter (ADR 0013): the `xterm.dart`, `core.dart`, `ui.dart`, `utils.dart`
  and `suggestion.dart` libraries, and xterm's value types `CellOffset`,
  `BufferRange`, `BufferSegment`, `CellData`, `CellAttr`, `CellColor`,
  `CellContent`, `NamedColor`, `CursorStyle`, `CursorPosition`,
  `TerminalState`, `TerminalTargetPlatform`, `MouseMode` and
  `MouseReportMode`, ported from xterm 4.0.0 (MIT).
